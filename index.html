Cheat sheets about Claude spread fast and age faster. The ones behind this site recommended models that have since been replaced, listed keyboard shortcuts that no longer work, and mixed one author's preferences in with facts.

Here, each claim is stamped with what checking it found, and each lesson links to the official page it was checked against. Your progress is saved only in this browser.
